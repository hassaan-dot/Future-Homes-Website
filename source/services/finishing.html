Complete Interior & Exterior Finishing Services

Finishing is where artistic elegance meets craftsmanship. At Future Homes, we transform basic grey structures into sophisticated, modern living spaces equipped with top-tier finishes and immaculate detail.

From precision porcelain floor tiling and marble installation to custom false ceilings, designer sanitary fittings, and hand-finished solid doors, our team delivers complete end-to-end finishing services tailored to your aesthetic tastes.

Key Finishing Capabilities

Tile & Marble Flooring

Laser-leveled installation of premium porcelain tiles, spanish ceramics, and polished marble flooring with perfect grouting.

Plaster & Wall Finishes

Multi-coat wall putty preparation, texture coatings, weather-shield exterior paint, and interior washable silk emulsion finishes.

Joinery & Woodwork

Solid Ash/Oak wood doors, customized modular kitchens, wardrobe units, vanity cabinets, and decorative wall panelling.

Ceiling, Lighting & Fixtures

Moisture-resistant gypsum ceiling designs, ambient cove LED lighting, premium switch plates, and luxury bathroom fittings.
