5 Essential Checklist Items for Residential Grey Structure Construction

Constructing a residential grey structure is the most critical investment in your home’s long-term safety and longevity. At Future Homes, we emphasize strict structural engineering standards to ensure your foundation and framing stand the test of time.

First and foremost, verifying the quality of steel reinforcement is paramount. Always ensure 100% certified 60-grade deformed steel bars are used for columns, beams, and slab binding. Substandard steel severely compromises seismic safety.

Second, concrete mixing ratios and slump tests must be logged daily. Whether using ready-mix or on-site mechanical mixing, maintaining precise cement-to-aggregate proportions ensures maximum compressive strength.

"A house is only as strong as its curing protocol. Continuous water curing for at least 14 days is essential to achieve full concrete curing strength."- Engr. Usman Chaudhry, Lead Structural Engineer

Finally, damp-proof membranes (DPC) and termite control treatments must be applied thoroughly at the substructure level prior to masonry work. Preventing capillary moisture rise early avoids costly wall damage during the finishing phase.
