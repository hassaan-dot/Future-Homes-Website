Grey Structure Construction Services

The grey structure is the backbone of any residential or commercial building. At Future Homes, we deliver robust, earthquake-resistant structural frameworks built strictly according to engineering standards and architect specs.

Our Grey Structure package encompasses every fundamental stage from ground excavation to slab casting, brickwork, and initial pipe conduits. We ensure high-density concrete mixes, certified 60-grade deformed steel reinforcement, and rigorous quality control at every pour to guarantee decades of structural safety.

Core Scope of Work

Excavation & Substructure

Site clearance, deep excavation, soil compaction, termiticides application, and RCC footings designed for heavy loads.

RCC Slab & Beam Framing

Certified 60-grade steel binding, precision formwork shuttering, and high-strength concrete casting for columns, beams, and slabs.

Masonry & Wall Construction

First-class clay brick masonry work with rich cement-sand mortar, damp-proof course (DPC), and lintel beam placement.

Concealed Conduits & Tanking

Underground and overhead water tanks with waterproofing, plus concealed electrical conduits and sanitary drainage lines.

Why Choose Our Grey Structure

We believe that a strong structure defines the lifespan of your property. Our site managers conduct daily inspections to ensure zero compromise on material ratios or structural integrity.

  • 100% Certified 60-Grade Steel Reinforcement
  • Lab-Tested Cement & Clean River Sand
  • Rigorous Curing Protocol (Minimum 14 Days)
  • Termite Proofing & Damp-Proof Membrane (DPC)
  • Seismic-Resistant Structural Detail & Supervision
  • On-Time Completion Milestones Guaranteed

Key Construction FAQs

Our Grey Structure package includes all concrete, 60-grade steel, first-class bricks, sand, crush, plumbing rough-ins, electrical piping, boundary wall, water tanks, and plaster work.

All structural designs are audited by licensed structural engineers according to local seismic zone regulations, featuring ductile detailing in column-beam joints and footings.

For a standard 10 Marla to 1 Kanal residential home, grey structure construction typically takes between 4 to 6 months depending on weather conditions and design complexity.
